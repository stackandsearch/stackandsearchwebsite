# Pricing research (November 2026)

The prices in `src/content/services/prices.ts` are **proposals**. They were set from the published Indian agency price guides below, for a studio selling to small and mid-sized businesses. Confirm or change each figure before launch; one edit updates the service page, the pricing page, the homepage packages and the structured data.

All figures are in rupees, before GST.

## Things to decide

- Whether the entry prices are too low for the work involved. They are set to be competitive, not to maximise margin.
- The minimum term for SEO, AEO and GEO plans. The site says a minimum term applies and is stated in the plan; it does not name a number.
- Payment stages. The site says a deposit to start and the balance at launch, without percentages.
- UI/UX design, speed optimisation, security, migration, integrations and AEO had no usable benchmark. Those figures are estimates and most need your judgement.
